Research

Nova3D

Co-author of Nova3D: Code-Native Generation of Programmable 3D Assets with Nimra Noor, Muhammad Bilal and Hassan Baig (arXiv 2607.22738, July 2026). Nova3D writes executable Blender code instead of a frozen mesh, so every text-to-3D or image-to-3D asset arrives with named parts, a real hierarchy, joints and edit handles.

Compared against eleven baselines from mesh, part-structured, code-native and CAD families. Code: github.com/RareSense/Nova3D · nova3d.xyz

FormaNova CAD Studio

From a photo to editable CAD

Together with Nimra Noor, I built one of the first pipelines that turns a reference image or a text description of a ring into editable, native Rhino CAD. The design is read, built procedurally, then validated and corrected automatically, so every stone, prong and band is its own object.

It powers FormaNova CAD Studio, Rare Sense's AI jewelry platform. The story is in my article From procedural rings to native Rhino CAD.
