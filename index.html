What I've made.

Research · 2026

Nova3D: Code-Native Generation of Programmable 3D Assets

Co-author with Nimra Noor, Muhammad Bilal and Hassan Baig (arXiv 2607.22738). Nova3D writes executable Blender code instead of a frozen mesh, so every generated object arrives with named parts, joints and edit handles. Its open-source client on GitHub has over 700 stars.

AI product · Rare Sense

FormaNova: image and text to editable jewelry CAD

With Nimra Noor, I built one of the first pipelines that turns a photo or a text description of a ring into editable, native Rhino CAD (.3dm), where every stone, prong and band is its own object. FormaNova also creates AI jewelry photoshoots.

Automation

AI automation for fashion brands

I built garment automation pipelines for the fashion brands J., Sitara and Kollective Moda. I train, deploy and automate AI systems end to end, from datasets and model training to production pipelines.

Robotics

Humanoid robot and robot simulation

A full humanoid design with 39 actuated joints built from real motors and servos, plus a quadruped robot dog simulated in MuJoCo inside 3D worlds generated with Nova3D.

Quick answers

About abdullahanxie.

Who is abdullahanxie?

abdullahanxie is Abdullah Hussain, an electrical engineering graduate from Rawalpindi, Pakistan. He is an AI engineer and researcher at Rare Sense Inc, co-author of the Nova3D paper on code-native 3D generation, and a filmmaker, documentary video editor, sound designer and athlete.

What has he built in AI and engineering?

At Rare Sense Inc he works on Nova3D (text-to-3D and image-to-3D generation of editable, part-aware 3D assets) and FormaNova (AI jewelry photoshoots and image/text-to-CAD). With Nimra Noor he built a pipeline that turns a photo or text description into editable native Rhino CAD. He has built garment automation pipelines for J., Sitara and Kollective Moda, and he designs and simulates robots, including a 39-joint humanoid.

Does he make videos?

Yes. He makes faceless YouTube documentaries and edits for channels and brands: business stories, true crime, history and science, with motion graphics, Blender 3D scenes and original sound design. The video portfolio is at abdullahanxie.com/video.
